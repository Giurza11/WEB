Top 5 Tips When Searching for a Car

  • 1. Define Your Budget

    Decide how much you can spend, including fuel, insurance and maintenance.

  • 2. Check Vehicle History

    Always check service records and past accidents before buying.

  • 3. Inspect the Car in Daylight

    It is easier to see scratches, dents and rust in natural light.

  • 4. Take a Test Drive

    Test the brakes, steering, acceleration and comfort on different roads.

  • 5. Compare Options

    Look at several cars before choosing the best one for your budget.

Types of Vehicles

  • 1. Sedan - A small to mid-size passenger car, usually with 4 doors.
  • 2. Hatchback - A compact car with a rear door that opens upward.
  • 3. SUV (Sport Utility Vehicle) - Larger car with higher ground clearance, often for off-road.
  • 4. Pickup Truck - Vehicle with a cargo bed for carrying goods.
  • 5. Coupe - Small, sporty 2-door car.
  • 6. Convertible - Car with a retractable roof.
  • 7. Van / Minivan - Spacious vehicle for passengers or cargo.
  • 8. Motorcycle - Two-wheeled motor vehicle.
  • 9. Electric Vehicle (EV) - Powered entirely by electricity.
  • 10. Hybrid Vehicle - Uses both gasoline and electric power.

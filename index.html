Get Scooting

Scooter sharing made simple

Scoot takes the hassle out of urban mobility. Our bikes are placed in convenient locations in each of our cities. Use our app to locate the nearest bike, unlock it with a tap, and you’re away!

Get Scooting

Locate with app

Use the app to find the nearest scooter to you. We are continuously placing scooters in the areas with most demand, so one should never be too far away.

Pick your scooter

We show the most important info for the scooters closest to you — like how much battery they have and roughly how much the ride will cost.

Enjoy the ride

Scan the QR code to unlock. Retract the cable lock, put on a helmet, and you're off! Always lock scooters away from walkways and accessibility ramps.
